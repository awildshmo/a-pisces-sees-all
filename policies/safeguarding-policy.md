# Safeguarding Policy — A Pisces Sees All

## Purpose

This policy sets out how A Pisces Sees All protects children, young people, and adults at risk who engage with our activities.

## Scope

Applies to all trustees, volunteers, practitioners, and anyone working on behalf of the organisation.

## Principles

- The welfare of the individual is paramount.
- All concerns will be taken seriously and acted upon promptly.
- Confidentiality will be maintained as far as is consistent with protecting the individual.

## Procedures

1. **Designated Safeguarding Lead**: One trustee will be appointed as the lead for safeguarding concerns.
2. **Reporting**: Any concern must be reported to the Safeguarding Lead immediately.
3. **Recording**: Concerns are logged securely with dates, times, and actions taken.
4. **Referral**: Where appropriate, concerns are referred to the Local Authority Designated Officer (LADO) or the police.
5. **DBS checks**: Practitioners and volunteers working regularly with children or adults at risk will undergo appropriate DBS checks.

## Training

Trustees and regular volunteers will receive basic safeguarding awareness training.

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the trustees.
