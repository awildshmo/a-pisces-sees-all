# Cookie Policy — A Pisces Sees All

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help the site function and, with your consent, help us understand how it is used.

## Types of Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies

Required for the website to work. These cannot be switched off.

- Session management
- Security and fraud prevention

### Analytics Cookies (optional)

Help us understand visitor behaviour so we can improve the site.

- Google Analytics (anonymised IP)

### Marketing Cookies (optional)

Used only if you have consented, e.g., for advertising or social media tracking.

## Managing Your Preferences

When you first visit, you will see a cookie banner letting you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time via the cookie settings link in the footer.

## Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services (e.g., Google). These are governed by the providers' own privacy policies.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us via the website.
