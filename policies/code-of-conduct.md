# Code of Conduct — A Pisces Sees All

## Our Commitment

A Pisces Sees All is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for everyone who engages with our work — whether through readings, workshops, meditation sessions, or online spaces.

## Expected Behaviour

- Treat all participants with respect and dignity.
- Be open to different beliefs, backgrounds, and experiences.
- Communicate honestly and kindly.
- Respect confidentiality: what is shared in a session stays in the session.
- Follow the guidance of session leaders and organisers.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind.
- Sexual harassment or unwelcome advances.
- Deliberate disruption of sessions or events.
- Sharing another person's private information without consent.
- Threats or violence.

## Confidentiality

Practitioners and organisers will not disclose personal information shared during sessions, except where required by law or where there is a serious risk of harm to the individual or others.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to the trustees or organisers as soon as possible. Reports will be handled promptly and confidentially.

## Enforcement

Trustees may take action including warnings, removal from sessions, or exclusion from future events, depending on the severity of the behaviour.
