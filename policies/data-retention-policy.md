# Data Retention Policy — A Pisces Sees All

## Purpose

This policy sets out how long A Pisces Sees All keeps personal data and when it is deleted, in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## Retention Schedule

| Data Type | Retention Period | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact details (name, email, phone) | Up to 6 years after last contact | Legal and accounting obligations |
| Booking records | Up to 6 years | Tax and legal records |
| Payment records | Up to 6 years | HMRC requirements |
| Marketing consent records | Until consent withdrawn | Accountability |
| Website analytics | Aggregated/anonymised after 26 months | Google Analytics default |
| Session notes (if kept) | Up to 2 years | Service improvement |
| Safeguarding records | Up to 25 years (or as advised) | Legal requirement |

## Deletion

When data reaches the end of its retention period, it is securely deleted or anonymised. Paper records are shredded; digital records are permanently erased.

## Review

This schedule is reviewed annually by the trustees.
