# Charitable Objects — A Pisces Sees All

Draft charitable objects for registration with the Charity Commission for England and Wales.

## Proposed Objects

1. **Advancement of education** for the public benefit in the study of astrology, tarot, and related spiritual and divinatory traditions, including their history, cultural significance, and symbolic systems, through the provision of free or affordable educational resources, public talks, workshops, and research.

2. **Advancement of religion** for the public benefit through the practice and promotion of contemplative and meditative traditions, including group meditation, for the spiritual development of the public.

3. **Relief of those in need** for the public benefit through the provision of free or low-cost contemplative and meditative support to those experiencing stress, anxiety, or emotional distress.

## Drafting Notes

- Objects must be *exclusively* charitable and for the public benefit.
- Paid one-to-one readings are commercial activity and must remain incidental to these purposes.
- The religion clause should be reviewed carefully — the Charity Commission expects a coherent belief system with devotional practice, not purely personal spirituality.
- Meditation is the strongest leg: well-established under education and relief-of-need.
- Tarot and astrology sit under education only.
