# Public Benefit Statement — A Pisces Sees All

## Benefit Aspect

The organisation's purposes are beneficial because:

- **Education**: Teaching the history, symbolism, and cultural context of astrology and tarot equips the public with knowledge and critical understanding of these traditions.
- **Religion / contemplation**: Group meditation and contemplative practice support spiritual development and wellbeing.
- **Relief of need**: Free or low-cost meditative support helps people experiencing stress, anxiety, or emotional distress.

## Public Aspect

- Activities are open to the general public, not restricted to members or a private group.
- Educational resources are free or affordable.
- Group meditation sessions are open to all who wish to attend.
- Any private benefit to trustees or founders is incidental and proportionate.

## Evidence to Maintain

- Attendance records for public events and workshops.
- Publication of educational materials (website, blog, resources).
- Feedback and testimonials from participants.
- Records showing that commercial activities (e.g., paid readings) are incidental to charitable purposes.
